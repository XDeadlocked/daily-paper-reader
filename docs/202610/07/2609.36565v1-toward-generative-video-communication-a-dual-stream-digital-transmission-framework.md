## 摘要
生成式视频通信在带宽受限的无线传输中已展现出潜力，并有望支持个性化内容交付。本文提出一种双流数字生成式视频通信（DGVC）框架，将传统数字链路与生成式链路相结合。传统链路提供基于信源的视觉参考，而生成式链路则传输紧凑的语义与感知信息，用于接收端生成。我们进一步讨论三种依赖带宽的运行模式，以及用于双流协调、同步、可靠性和时延控制的关键技术。一个实际案例研究展示了 DGVC 在无线衰落信道下的感知质量和时序质量优势。最后，我们讨论生成式视频通信的开放挑战与未来研究方向。

## Abstract
Generative video communication has shown promise for bandwidth-constrained wireless transmission and has the potential to support personalized content delivery. In this article, we propose a dual-stream digital generative video communication (DGVC) framework that integrates a traditional digital link with a generative link. The traditional link provides source-grounded visual references, while the generative link conveys compact semantic and perceptual information for receiver-side generation. We further discuss three bandwidth-dependent operating regimes and key technologies for dual-stream coordination, synchronization, reliability, and latency control. A practical case study demonstrates the perceptual and temporal-quality benefits of DGVC under wireless fading channels. Finally, we discuss open challenges and future research directions for generative video communication.
